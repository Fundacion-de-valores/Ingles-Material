🎯 Learning Objectives #

At the end of the class, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Learn the different prepositions of time and know when to use them.
  • ✅ Use the different prepositions of place.
  • ✅ Use demonstrative adjectives to point and refer to objects.
  • ✅ Learn new vocabulary about navigating online.
  • ✅ Describe the way of doing something using adverbs of manner.

📖 Introduction #

Today you will learn new vocabulary about technology. At the end of the class, you will know how to use prepositions of time and place. You will also know how to refer to objects that are near or far from you. Finally, you will be able to describe how something is done.

📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary #

🔵 1. Prepositions of Time #

We have 3 different prepositions of time: at, in, and on.

⏰ AT — Specific time or holiday period #

Use Example
Specific time I get up at 7 o’clock.
Specific time The movie starts at 8:30.
Holiday period They sing carols at Christmas.
Holiday period Come and visit us at Thanksgiving.

📅 ON — Days of the week and specific dates #

Use Example
Day of the week I will visit you on Wednesday.
Day of the week Where were you on Friday?
Specific date His birthday is on March 27th.
Specific date The exam is on the 16th.

📆 IN — Months, years, decades, centuries, seasons & time periods #

Use Example
Month My birthday is in November.
Year The Titanic sank in 1912.
Decade There were many hippies in the ’60s.
Century We are living in the 21st century.
Season We go to the beach in summer.
Time period The meeting starts in 10 minutes.

💡 Quick reference:

Preposition Use for
AT Specific times & holidays
ON Days & specific dates
IN Months, years, seasons, centuries

🟢 2. Prepositions of Place #

We use prepositions of place to indicate the location or position of an object, person, or place.

They normally follow 2 sentence patterns:

Pattern Structure Example
Pattern 1 The + noun + verb + preposition + the + noun The cat is on the sofa.
Pattern 2 There + verb + a/an + noun + preposition + the + noun There is a cat on the sofa. / There are two cats on the sofa.

Most common prepositions of place:

Preposition Meaning Example
in inside The book is in the bag.
on on top of / on a surface The cup is on the table.
under below The cat is under the chair.
behind at the back The ball is behind the door.
in front of facing She is in front of the class.
next to / beside at the side The lamp is next to the bed.
between in the middle of two things The bank is between the school and the park.
above higher than The clock is above the TV.
below lower than The shoes are below the shelf.

🟡 3. Demonstrative Adjectives #

Demonstrative adjectives are used to identify nouns based on their distance from the speaker. There are 4: this, these, that, those.

Near (can see, touch & point) Far (can see & point, but NOT touch)
Singular THIS THAT
Plural THESE THOSE

👆 THIS — Singular, near #

"This is my cat." (I can see it, touch it, and point to it.) "This dog is big."

👆👆 THESE — Plural, near #

"These apples are red." (I can see them, touch them, and point to them.) "These are my brothers."

👉 THAT — Singular, far #

"That car over there is mine." (I can see it and point to it, but I can’t touch it.) "That is my mom."

👉👉 THOSE — Plural, far #

"Those notebooks on the table are ours." (I can see them and point to them, but I can’t touch them.) "Those are big trees."

💡 In English, demonstrative adjectives can refer to people too — it is not considered impolite.

🔴 5. Adverbs of Manner #

We use adverbs of manner to describe a verb (an action) — how somebody does something.

“He drives carefully.” “My mother speaks slowly.” “The nurses left the room quietly.”

5.1 How to form an adverb of manner #

Normally, add -ly to an adjective:

Adjective Adverb
happy happily
careful carefully
quick quickly
slow slowly
beautiful beautifully
sad sadly

Here you will find a list of the most common adverbs of manner:

5.2 Irregular Adverbs — must be memorized! #

Adjective Adverb
good well
hard hard
fast fast
late late
straight straight
high high

⚠️ These adverbs do not add -ly — they keep the same form or change completely.

5.3 Adjectives vs. Adverbs #

Type Describes Example
Adjective A noun (usually with verb “to be”) She is beautiful.
Adverb An action / verb She sings beautifully.

💡 Adjectives describe what something is. Adverbs describe how something is done.

🔑 Key Concepts — Quick Reference #

Topic Key Rule
Prepositions of Time at (specific time/holidays) / on (days & dates) / in (months, years, seasons)
Prepositions of Place Indicate location: in, on, under, behind, next to, between…
Demonstrative Adjectives this/these (near) / that/those (far) — singular/plural
Online Vocabulary Scroll, click, log in, sign up, shut down, delete…
Adverbs of Manner Add -ly to adjective / memorize irregulars: well, hard, fast, late

💡 Examples #

Prepositions of Time #

“The class starts at 9:00.” “We have an exam on Monday.” “School begins in September.”

Prepositions of Place #

“The book is on the desk.” “There is a lamp next to the window.”

Demonstrative Adjectives #

"This is my phone." (near) "Those people over there are my friends." (far)

Adverbs of Manner #

“She works hard every day.” (irregular) “He explained the lesson clearly.” “They spoke quietly in the library.”

✏️ Practical Exercises #

Exercise 1 — AT, ON, or IN? #

Fill in the blank with the correct preposition:

“The party starts ___ 8:00.” → at “My birthday is ___ July.” → in “We have class ___ Monday.” → on “She was born ___ 2001.” → in “They always eat together ___ Christmas.” → at

Exercise 2 — Prepositions of Place #

Describe where objects are in your room using at least 5 prepositions of place:

“My phone is on the table. My bag is under the chair. My books are next to the door…”

Exercise 3 — This, These, That, or Those? #

Choose the correct demonstrative adjective:

"___ book here is mine." → This "___ mountains over there are beautiful." → Those "___ are my keys." (holding them) → These "___ building far away is the school." → That

Exercise 4 — Adverb or Adjective? #

Fill in the blank with the correct form:

“She is a ___ (careful) driver.” → careful (adjective) “She drives ___.” → carefully (adverb) “He is ___.” (good) → good (adjective) “He plays soccer ___.” (good) → well (adverb)

Exercise 5 — Speaking Activity #

Describe your daily routine using:

  • At least 2 prepositions of time
  • At least 2 prepositions of place
  • At least 3 adverbs of manner

🎙️ Example: “I wake up at 6:00. My phone is on my desk. I eat breakfast quickly. I walk to school slowly because I live far. I study carefully every night.”

Record yourself and share with the class!

📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember #

Before you go, make sure you know:

  1. Prepositions of Time — AT (specific times & holidays) / ON (days & dates) / IN (months, years, seasons, centuries).
  2. Prepositions of Place — in, on, under, behind, next to, between, in front of, above, below.
  3. Demonstrative Adjectives — This / These (near) / That / Those (far). Singular vs. plural.
  4. Adverbs of Manner — Add -ly to adjectives. Memorize irregulars: well, hard, fast, late, straight, high.

Keep practicing every day — little by little you will sound more natural! 🌟
