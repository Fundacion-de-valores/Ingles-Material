🕰️ Simple Past Verbs #

We use this tense to talk about completed actions that happened in the past.

There are 2 types of verbs in Simple Past:

Type How to form Examples
Regular verbs Add -ed or -d at the end walk → walked / play → played / want → wanted
Irregular verbs Change their form — must be memorized eat → ate / see → saw / go → went

🟢 2. Simple Present Review #

Access the platform materials and review these key points:

Focus Reminder
3rd person Add -s / -es / -ies → She walks. / He goes. / It cries.
Questions Does + subject + base verb → Does he eat breakfast?
Negatives Do/Does + not + base verb → She doesn’t wake up late.
Example “She always wakes up early. Does he eat breakfast?”

🟡 3. Simple Past — ED Pronunciation #

When using regular verbs in the past tense, the -ed ending has 3 different sounds:

🔴 /ɪd/ — sounds like “id” #

Used when the verb ends in the sound /t/ or /d/

Base verb Past form
visit visited
want wanted
decide decided

⚫ /t/ — sounds like “t” #

Used when the verb ends in the voiceless sounds: k, s, ch, sh, x, p

Base verb Past form
walk walked
cook cooked
fix fixed
watch watched
wash washed

🔵 /d/ — sounds like “d” #

Used when the verb ends in the voiced sounds: l, n, r, g, v, b, m

Base verb Past form
play played
love loved
offer offered
clean cleaned

💡 Quick rule summary:

Ending sound ED pronunciation Example
/t/ or /d/ /ɪd/ wanted
Voiceless (k,s,ch,sh,x,p) /t/ walked
Voiced (l,n,r,g,v,b,m) /d/ played

📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember #

Before you go, make sure you know:

  1. Rules — No Spanish, camera on, participate actively. Use magic questions whenever you need help.
  2. Simple Present Review — Use do/does + base form for questions and negatives. Don’t forget 3rd person -s/-es/-ies.
  3. Simple Past — Regular verbs — Add -ed and pronounce it correctly: /t/ / /d/ / /ɪd/ depending on the ending sound.
  4. Simple Past — Irregular verbs — There are no rules, you must memorize them. Practice the 80 most common ones with Quizlet.

Keep practicing every day — little by little you will sound more natural! 🌟
