🎯 Learning Objectives #

At the end of the class, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Read and say time in American English (“Ten o-three”, “Twelve o’clock”).
  • ✅ Reinforce daily routine vocabulary and frequency adverbs from Day 3.
  • ✅ Learn American mailing address format and state abbreviations.
  • ✅ Practice giving professional opinions, agreements, and disagreements for call center skills.

📖 Introduction #

Today you will review all the topics from the week — routines, frequency adverbs, 3rd person rule, and pronouns — while adding practical skills: time-telling, addresses, and opinion phrases. As an A1 student, you must master these for work schedules, directions, customer service, or daily planning conversations.

📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary #

Term Definition
Telling Time (American style) “Ten o-three” (10:03); “quarter past” and “half past” are rare in American English
Street Types St. (Street), Ave. (Avenue), Rd. (Road), Blvd. (Boulevard)
State Abbreviations 50 abbreviations — CA (California), NY (New York), TX (Texas)
Mailing Address Order Name → Street # + Name + Apt # → City, State ZIP → USA
Giving Opinions Phrases like “In my opinion,” “I agree,” “I disagree,” “As far as I’m concerned”

🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation #

🔵 1. Telling Time #

Time How to say it
10:00 “It’s ten o’clock.”
10:03 “It’s ten o-three.” (add “o-” for minutes 1–9)
10:13 “It’s ten thirteen.”
10:30 “It’s ten thirty.”
10:32 “It’s ten thirty-two.”

💡 For minutes 1–9, always say “o-” before the number: 5:07 → “five o-seven” ⚠️ Avoid “quarter past” or “half past” — American English prefers numbers: “ten thirty” not “half past ten”

🟢 2. Daily Routine + Frequency (Review) #

The natural sequence of a daily routine:

Step Action
1 Wake up
2 Brush teeth
3 Eat / Have breakfast ❌ take breakfast
4 Go to work / school

Frequency adverb placement:

✅ She always wakes up at six. ✅ He never skips breakfast.

How often…?

“How often does she eat breakfast?” → “She eats breakfast every day.”

🟡 3. Mailing Addresses #

American mailing address format:

Line Content Example
1 Full name John Doe
2 Street number + name + Apt # 123 Main St. Apt 4B
3 City, State abbreviation ZIP code Miami, FL 33101
4 Country USA

Common street abbreviations:

Full word Abbreviation
Street St.
Avenue Ave.
Road Rd.
Boulevard Blvd.

Common state abbreviations:

State Abbreviation
California CA
New York NY
Texas TX
Florida FL
Illinois IL

🟠 4. Giving Opinions #

Type Phrases
Strong agreement “Absolutely! You’re right.”
Mild agreement “I see your point.” / “I think so too.”
Opinion “In my opinion…” / “I believe…” / “As far as I’m concerned…”
Mild disagreement “I’m not sure about that.” / “I think otherwise.”
Strong disagreement “I disagree.” / “That’s not true.”

🎙️ Tip: Your intonation rises when you express opinions. Practice sounding natural and confident!

💡 Examples #

Telling Time #

“What time is it?” → “It’s two fifteen.” → “It’s five o-seven.” → “It’s eleven thirty.”

Daily Routine #

“She always wakes up at six o’clock, eats breakfast, and goes to work.”

Mailing Address #

John Doe 123 Main St. Apt 4B Miami, FL 33101 USA

Giving Opinions #

“In my opinion, Coca-Cola is delicious.” “I agree!” / “I disagree.”

Combined #

“I wake up at seven o’clock every day. My address is 456 Oak Ave., Dallas, TX 75201. In my opinion, it’s a great routine. I agree!”

✏️ Practical Exercises #

Exercise 1 — Telling Time #

Write or say the time out loud:

Clock Your answer
3:05 “Three o-five.”
9:22 “Nine twenty-two.”
7:00 "Seven o’clock."
11:08 “Eleven o-eight.”

🎯 Practice 10 different clocks with a partner and take turns asking “What time is it?”

Exercise 2 — Mailing Address #

Format this information as a proper American mailing address:

Name: Maria Lopez | Street: 789 Pine Road, Apt 12 | City: Dallas | State: Texas | ZIP: 75201

Write the full address in the correct order:

_______________________
_______________________
_______________________, __ _____
USA

(Answer: Maria Lopez / 789 Pine Rd. Apt 12 / Dallas, TX 75201 / USA)

Exercise 3 — Routine + Frequency #

Fill in the blanks:

“My boss ___ (always) arrives at eight o’clock.” “How often ___ you ___ (check) email?” → do / check “She ___ brushes her teeth in the morning.” → always / usually

Exercise 4 — Opinion Debate #

Topic: “Classes start too early.”

  1. Respond with 3 agreement phrases.
  2. Respond with 3 disagreement phrases.
  3. Switch topic: “English is easy.”

🎙️ Record yourself for 1 minute. Listen back and note your intonation — does it rise when you give opinions?

📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember #

Before you go, make sure you know:

  1. Time — Use “o-” for minutes 1–9 (“five o-seven”); say numbers directly for 10+ (“ten thirteen”). Avoid “quarter/half past” in American English.
  2. Routine — Wake up → eat breakfast (not take) → work → rest. Place frequency adverbs before the main verb.
  3. Address — Name → Street # + name → City, ST ZIP → USA. Learn the 50 state abbreviations and street types (St., Ave., Rd., Blvd.).
  4. Opinions — Use “I agree / I disagree,” “In my opinion,” “I believe,” “Some people say that” with rising intonation for natural-sounding English.

Great work finishing Week 1! You are building a strong foundation — keep practicing every day! 🌟
