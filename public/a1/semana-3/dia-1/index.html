🎯 Learning Objectives #

At the end of the class, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Use the simple future with “will” to make predictions, decisions, and promises.
  • ✅ Use “be going to” to talk about plans and intentions.
  • ✅ Identify and use common future signal words (tomorrow, next week, soon).
  • ✅ Recognize and use basic future clauses in sentences (when, if, before, after).

📖 Introduction #

Today you will review key future forms, including “will”, “be going to”, future signal words, and basic future clauses. You will practice how to talk about plans, predictions, and decisions in real-life situations. As an A1 student, mastering these structures will help you communicate about future events in contexts such as scheduling, making arrangements, and basic workplace interactions.

📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary #

Term Definition
Simple Future with “Will” Used for predictions, instant decisions, and promises (I will help you. / It will rain.)
“Be Going To” Used for plans and intentions (I am going to study. / They are going to travel.)
Future Signal Words Words that indicate future time (tomorrow, next week, soon, later, tonight)
Future Clauses Words used to connect ideas about the future (when, if, before, after)
Time Expressions Phrases used to talk about upcoming events (in two days, next month, this weekend)

🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation #

🔵 1. Simple Future with “Will” #

Used for predictions, instant decisions, and promises.

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + will + base verb I will help you.
Negative Subject + will not / won’t + base verb It won’t rain today.
Interrogative Will + subject + base verb? Will you help me?

💡 Use “will” when you decide something at the moment of speaking: “The phone is ringing. I will answer it.”

🟢 2. “Be Going To” #

Used for plans and intentions made before speaking.

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + am/is/are going to + base verb I am going to study tonight.
Negative Subject + am/is/are not going to + base verb She is not going to travel.
Interrogative Am/Is/Are + subject + going to + base verb? Are they going to start?

💡 Use “be going to” when the plan was already decided: “I am going to visit my family this weekend.” (you decided earlier)

🟡 3. Future Signal Words #

Words that indicate future time — they tell you when something will happen:

Signal Word Example
Tomorrow I will call you tomorrow.
Next week / month / year She is going to travel next week.
Soon He will finish the project soon.
Later I will help you later.
Tonight We are going to study tonight.
In + time They will arrive in two days.

🟠 4. Future Clauses #

Used to connect two ideas about the future using when, if, before, after.

⚠️ Important rule: After when / if / before / after, use present simple — NOT “will”!

Clause word Correct ✅ Incorrect ❌
When When I arrive, I will call you. When I will arrive…
If If it rains, I will stay home. If it will rain…
Before Before I go to bed, I will study. Before I will go…
After After she finishes, she will rest. After she will finish…

🔑 The rule: Time clause (present simple) + , + main clause (will)

💡 Examples #

Will — Predictions / Instant Decisions #

“I will call you later.” “It will rain tomorrow.” “Don’t worry, I will help you.”

Be Going To — Plans / Intentions #

“I am going to study tonight.” “She is going to visit her family next weekend.” “They are going to start a new job soon.”

Future Signal Words #

“I will travel next month.” “We are going to meet tomorrow.” “He will finish the project soon.”

Future Clauses #

"When I finish work, I will call you." "If it rains, I will stay home." "Before I go to bed, I will study English."

✏️ Practical Exercises #

Exercise 1 — Will (Decisions / Predictions) #

Complete the sentences with the correct form of “will”:

Sentence Answer
I ___ (help) you with that. I will help you.
It ___ (rain) tomorrow. It will rain tomorrow.

🎯 Make 3 predictions about the future and share them with a partner.

Exercise 2 — Be Going To (Plans / Intentions) #

Complete with am / is / are going to:

Sentence Answer
She ___ ___ ___ study tonight. She is going to study tonight.
They ___ ___ ___ travel next week. They are going to travel next week.

🎯 Talk with a partner about 3 plans for the weekend using “be going to”.

Exercise 3 — Future Signal Words #

Choose the correct option:

“I will call you (tomorrow / yesterday).” → tomorrow “We are going to meet (next week / last week).” → next week

🎯 Write 3 sentences using three different future signal words.

Exercise 4 — Future Clauses #

Complete the sentences with the correct verb form:

Sentence Answer
When I ___ (finish) work, I will call you. When I finish work…
If it ___ (rain), I will stay home. If it rains, I will stay home.

🎯 Create 2 sentences using “when” and 2 sentences using “if”.

Exercise 5 — Speaking Practice (Combined) #

Topic: Your plans for the weekend

Use at least:

  • 1 sentence with will
  • 1 sentence with be going to
  • 1 sentence with a future clause

Example: “If I have time, I will go out. I am going to visit my family.”

📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember #

Before you go, make sure you know:

  1. Will — Use for predictions, instant decisions, and promises. Structure: will + base verb.
  2. Be Going To — Use for plans and intentions already decided. Structure: am/is/are + going to + base verb.
  3. Future Signal Words — Tomorrow, next week, soon, later, tonight, in + time expression signal the future.
  4. Future Clauses — After when / if / before / after, use present simple (NOT will). The main clause uses will.

⚠️ Common mistake to avoid: ❌ When I will arrive, I will call you. ✅ When I arrive, I will call you.
