🎯 Learning Objectives #

At the end of the class, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Use the present continuous to describe actions happening now.
  • ✅ Use the present continuous to talk about future arrangements.
  • ✅ Identify time expressions related to present and future (now, right now, tonight, tomorrow).
  • ✅ Describe personal plans and scheduled activities using appropriate structure and vocabulary.

📖 Introduction #

Today you will learn and practice the present continuous for actions happening now and for future arrangements. You will focus on how to describe current activities and talk about planned events in real-life situations. As an A1 student, mastering these structures will help you communicate effectively in contexts such as scheduling, making plans, and basic workplace or customer service interactions.

📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary #

Term Definition
Present Continuous (Now) Used for actions happening at the moment of speaking (I am studying. / She is working.)
Present Continuous (Future Arrangements) Used for fixed plans in the near future (I am meeting my boss tomorrow.)
Structure Subject + am/is/are + verb -ing
Time Expressions (Now) now, right now, at the moment
Time Expressions (Future) tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, next week
Common Verbs working, studying, meeting, calling, traveling, eating, preparing

🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation #

🔵 1. Present Continuous — Actions Happening Now #

Used for actions happening at the moment of speaking.

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + am/is/are + verb -ing I am studying now.
Negative Subject + am/is/are not + verb -ing She is not working right now.
Interrogative Am/Is/Are + subject + verb -ing? Are they eating?

💡 Signal words for “now”: now, right now, at the moment, currently, look! / listen!

🟢 2. Present Continuous — Future Arrangements #

Used for fixed plans in the near future — usually with a specific time or person.

Form Example
Affirmative I am meeting my boss tomorrow.
Negative She is not traveling tonight.
Interrogative Are you working this weekend?

💡 The plan was already arranged before the moment of speaking — it is not a spontaneous decision. Compare: “I will help you.” (decision NOW) vs. “I am meeting her tomorrow.” (arranged BEFORE)

🟡 3. Forming -ing Verbs #

Rule When to use Examples
+ ing Most verbs work → working / eat → eating
Drop -e + ing Verbs ending in -e make → making / have → having
Double consonant + ing Short verbs ending in consonant-vowel-consonant run → running / sit → sitting

⚠️ Never add -ing to a verb without am / is / are: ❌ I studying → ✅ I am studying

🟠 4. Time Expressions #

When Expressions
Now now, right now, at the moment, currently
Future tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, on Monday

💡 Examples #

Present Continuous — Now #

“I am studying right now.” “She is working at the moment.” “They are eating lunch now.”

Present Continuous — Future Arrangements #

“I am meeting my boss tomorrow.” “She is traveling next week.” “We are having a meeting tonight.”

Time Expressions #

“I am working now.” “They are studying tonight.” “He is calling you tomorrow.”

-ing Verb Forms #

“She is making dinner.” (make → making) “He is running in the park.” (run → running) “We are studying English.” (study → studying)

📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember #

Before you go, make sure you know:

  1. Present Continuous (Now) — am/is/are + verb(-ing) for actions happening now. (I am working now.)
  2. Present Continuous (Future) — Used for planned arrangements with a specific time. (I am meeting her tomorrow.)
  3. Time Expressions — Now → now, right now, at the moment; Future → tonight, tomorrow, next week.
  4. -ing Form — Add -ing (work → working), drop -e (make → making), double consonant (run → running).

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid: ❌ I am study now. → ✅ I am studying now. ❌ I meeting my friend tomorrow. → ✅ I am meeting my friend tomorrow.
