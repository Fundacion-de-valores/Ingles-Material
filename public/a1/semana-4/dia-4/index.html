🎯 Learning Objectives #

At the end of the class, you will be able to:

  • ✅ Give instructions explaining complete procedures using sequence words.
  • ✅ Give commands, orders, or tell people what to do or what they need to do.

📖 Introduction #

Today you will learn how to explain procedures you have to follow in order to do something. You will also be able to use words and use the correct structure of sentences to give orders, commands, or tell other people what to do.

📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary #

📌 Giving Directions / Instructions #

This topic will allow you to explain step by step how to perform an action or complete a procedure. Here are some words that will help you during the process:

🟢 Starting Out #

Expression
Before you begin, (you should…)
The first thing you do is…
I would start by…
The best place to begin is…
First / Firstly…
First of all…

🔵 Continuing #

Expression
After that…
The next step is to…
The next thing you do is…
Once you’ve done that, then…
When you finish that, then…
To begin with…

🔴 Finishing #

Expression
The last step is…
The last thing you do is…
In the end…
When you’ve finished…
When you’ve completed all the steps…

📌 Imperative Sentences #

This type of sentence is used to give commands, orders, invitations, or instructions. We basically use them to tell people what to do.

Sentence Structure → Verb + Complement

These sentences don’t need a subject in their structure. To make them more polite you can use the words “please” and “thank you”.

For example:

Sentence
Sit down.
Read the next part please.
Come here!
Remember to do your homework.

We often use imperatives in positive and negative sentences:

Positive Negative
Come here! Don’t talk to strangers.
Eat your vegetables. Don’t eat fast food.
Be on time. Don’t be late.
Listen to me. Don’t ignore me.
Go outside and play. Don’t play video games all day.
Clean up your room. Don’t leave your clothes on the floor.
Please be quiet. Don’t bother others.

🔑 Key Concepts #

  • Sequence Words help us to guide someone through a process step by step.
  • Imperative Sentences help us to give instructions, commands, or orders to someone else.

Examples #

In order to prepare a lemon pie, follow these instructions:

First grind the cookies into fine crumbs, then add the melted butter and compact it in the pyrex. For the mixture, put the cream, cream cheese, condensed milk and a cup of lemon juice in the blender, after that mix it until smooth. Finally put the mixture in the pyrex and let it freeze for 3 hours.

Follow the instructions given in class:

a. Listen to the audio.

b. Answer the questions.

c. Compare it with a partner.

d. Send it to the teacher to review.

📝 Summary #

  • Use sequence words to explain processes.
  • Use imperative sentences to give orders or commands.
