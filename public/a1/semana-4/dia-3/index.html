📖 Introduction #

This is a practice material to help you study and get ready for your certification test. Here, you will find all the topics you learned throughout the month with your teachers. This material includes exercises in preparation for your certification test, make sure to be conscious and practice everything you can in order to feel prepared for your test. Don’t forget to be honest when you do the exercises included.

The certification week is an important week, don’t see it as a vacation week. This material will help you if you use it and your time wisely. Although this is a self-practice material you can always feel free to ask your teachers if you have questions while practicing. Please read and follow the instructions carefully, that will also help you in your certification test.

Let’s get ready!! You can do it!!

Section 3 #

🎯 Goal: describe your or someone else’s daily routine, use the simple present to express habits, events, or repeated actions.

Task 1 – Writing: write 6 sentences about your daily routine/habits.

For example: I wake up at 8am.

Task 2 – Speaking: choose a person from your family and say his/her daily routine out loud. Don’t forget to use the rules of the 3rd person singular.

Task 3 – Fill in the blanks: complete the sentences with the correct negative form. Don’t forget to use the auxiliary verbs for the simple present tense:

  1. I __________ (not/eat) junk food.
  2. Dogs __________ (not/like) to chase mice.
  3. Ester __________ (not/sing) in the shower.
  4. My dad __________ (not/clean) the house on the weekends.
  5. We __________ (not/like) to travel.
  6. It __________ (not/rain) here in January.

Task 4 – Unscramble: put the words in the correct order to form questions in the simple present tense. Make sure to use the auxiliary verbs for questions and add a question mark (they are not included).

  1. like / pizza / to / eat / you
  2. mom / your / every / cook / day
  3. the / play / children / the / on / soccer / weekend
  4. Daniel / siblings / have
  5. cloudy / it / today

Task 5 – Frequency Adverbs: complete the sentences using a frequency adverb according to the context.

  1. I __________ take a bus to class, maybe three or four days a week.

  3. Judy saw a doctor for the third time in a month. She __________ gets sick.
  4. I get up at five o’clock seven days a week. I __________ get up early.
  5. It’s always hot and sunny where I live. That’s why I __________ see snow.

🔑 Extra Tips for your interview #

  • Open your mouth and speak clearly.
  • Dress formally, remember this is a preparation for a real job interview.
  • Have a good attitude during your interview, smile and use a good tone of voice.
  • Keep eye contact all the time, it is important to show you are confident.
  • Take your feedback and put it into practice, be humble with the things you need to improve and continue practicing, you’re doing a great job!
