Learning Objectives: #

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  1. Use the past of the verb be (was/were) with time expressions to describe past customer issues and interactions clearly.
  2. Ask and answer questions using Wh- question words in correct simple past question structures.

Introduction: #

Today, you will learn how to talk about past experiences or states using the verb “be”. This includes describing events and interactions, asking questions to understand past issues, and giving clear information about previous situations. We will focus on using the past of the verb be (was/were), simple past verbs with time expressions, and forming questions using Wh- words and correct structures. These skills will help you communicate more effectively, ask better questions, and get ready to use them in a call center environment. By the end of the lesson, you’ll be able to confidently talk about past events and handle customer conversations related to previous issues.

Important terms (Vocabulary): #

Simple Past Time Expressions:

  • Yesterday
  • Last week
  • Last month
  • Last year
  • Last Friday (Monday, Tuesday, etc…)
  • An hour ago
  • Recently
  • A little while ago
  • In the past

Question Words:* #

  1. What → asks about a thing 🥗📱🚗
    • What did you have for breakfast?
  2. Where → asks about a place 🏡🏫🏥
    • Where did you live last year?
  3. When → asks about time ⏰⏳
    • When did you finish your homework?
  4. Who → asks about a person 🧍🏻
    • Who was your teacher in Elementary?
  5. Why → asks for a reason 🤷🏻‍♀️
    • Why were you late?
  6. How → asks about the way or method 🛠️⚙️
    • How did you go to work?

Key Concepts (Grammar Rules): #

Verb “Be” in the Simple Past:

The verb “be” in the simple past is used to talk about states, situations, or conditions in the past (not actions). It has 2 forms: Was and Were. We use them as it shows in the chart below:

Personal Pronouns Verb “Be”
I / HE / SHE / IT Was
YOU / WE / YOU GUYS / THEY WERE

Examples:

  • I was tired yesterday.
  • She was at work.
  • They were happy.
Affirmative
I was sad.
You were at home.
He was a doctor.
Ashley was a doctor.
My doy was hungry.
We were in New York.
You guys were dancers.
They were great friends.
Negative
I wasn’t happy.
You weren’t at the party.
Tom wasn’t okay.
She wasn’t a lawyer.
It wasn’t sleepy.
My sister and I weren’t singers.
You guys weren’t singers.
Mom and dad weren’t coworkers
Closed Questions
Was I drunk?
Were you at the mall?
Was he sad?
Was she a dentist?
Was it okay?
Were we in our country?
Were you guys happy?
Were they a couple?

Key Notes for Students: #

❌ Do NOT use “did” with was/were #

❌ Did you were at work? #

✔️ Were you at work? #

The verb does not change (no -ing, no base form)

❌ I was go to work #

✔️ I was at work #

Time Expressions: #

You can use time expressions in your simple past sentences to specify a time. Examples:

  • I was sad last night.
  • My friends were at a party yesterday.
  • Last year, we were in New York. They can also be used with action verbs. Examples:
  • I had a meeting an hour ago.
  • Recently, we moved to Paris.

Simple Past Question Structures: #

As you learned yesterday, the auxiliary we use with the simple past is “Did”. When asking questions, you will need to use this auxiliary + the base form of a verb to structure a question.

IMPORTANT REMINDER: We do NOT use “Did” with the verb to be, we only use it with action verbs.

Structures:

Closed Ended Questions(Yes/No)
Auxiliary Subject Verb Complement
Did You Study for the test?
Did he participate in class?
Did we learn this topic?
Did they pass the certification?
Open Ended Questions
Question Word Auxiliary Subject Verb Complement
Where did you study for the test?
Why did he participate in class?
When did we learn this topic?
How did they pass the certification?

📝 Summary: #

Verb “Be” (was/were): Used for past states, situations, and conditions (not actions).

  • Was: I / He / She / It
  • Were: You / We / They

Structures: #

✔️ Affirmative: Subject + was/were #

✔️ Negative: Subject + wasn’t/weren’t #

✔️ Questions: Was/Were + subject? #

❌ Do NOT use did with was/were #

Time Expressions: #

Used to show when (yesterday, last night, ago, etc.)

Simple Past Questions (Action Verbs):

✔️ Yes/No: Did + subject + base verb? #

✔️ Wh-: Wh-word + did + subject + base verb? #
