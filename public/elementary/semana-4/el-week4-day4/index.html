Day 4 – Week 4 #

Topics of the Day: #

  • A1 Self-Study Practice Guide (Certification Prep)

Learning Objectives: By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  1. Produce simple spoken sentences to talk about personal information, descriptions, daily routines, past activities, and free time using basic vocabulary and structures.
  2. Demonstrate basic pronunciation skills by clearly spelling words, reading numbers and dates, and pronouncing common words and endings (e.g., -s, -ed).
  3. Read short sentences and simple information aloud with understandable pronunciation, appropriate pauses, and correct expansion of abbreviations (e.g., addresses).

Introduction: #

Welcome to your pre-certification day. Today’s focus is to help you get ready for your certification interview and written test. The exercises below are designed to help you review and expand your vocabulary so that you can answer the questions clearly and confidently. Please work on this guide carefully and thoughtfully. The more effort you put into building your answers correctly, the better prepared you will be for your certification. Follow the instructions in this guide as they are. If it asks you to say or read something aloud, please do it, even if you are alone. Practicing out loud will help build your confidence. This guide is designed for independent work, as you should already have the knowledge to complete it on your own and even notice some of your mistakes. However, you can always ask your teacher for guidance if you have questions about any section. Take advantage of this time to prepare, and enjoy!

PART 1: PERSONAL INFORMATION (Speaking Practice) #

🎯 Goal: Talk about yourself using simple sentences Step 1: Complete the model Fill in your information:

  • My name is __________.
  • I am from __________.
  • I am __________ years old.
  • I live in __________.

Step 2: Add one extra sentence Choose ONE:

  • I am a student.
  • I work in __________.
  • I like __________.
  • I enjoy ________.
  • I don’t like _______.

Step 3: Speak (3 times)

Read your full introduction OUT LOUD 3 times. Use Natural Reader if you have any doubts about the pronunciation. https://www.naturalreaders.com/online/ . Once you’re ready, use the “voice to text” option in your phone to dictate the sentences, if your phone writes them correctly, your pronunciation is good, if it doesn’t, check on natural reader again, and repeat as many times as necessary, until you can say it without reading.

PART 2: DESCRIBING A PLACE OR A PERSON #

🎯 Goal: Describe a place or a person using basic vocabulary Step 1: Complete the sentences

  • My country is __________ (name).
  • It is __________ (big/small/beautiful/colorful/fun/relaxing/noisy).
  • The weather is __________ (hot/cold/cloudy/sunny).
  • The people are __________ (friendly/nice/polite/rude/welcoming).
  • My mom’s name is _______.
  • She is ________ (kind/nice/patient/organized/protective)
  • She has _______ (blond, dark, curly, straight) hair.
  • She is _______ years old.

Step 2: Add one more idea

  • In my country, we have __________ (food/place/music).
  • My mom is a/an _________ (profession).

Step 3: Speak (2–3 times) Read all sentences together. Use Natural Reader if you have any doubts about the pronunciation. https://www.naturalreaders.com/online/

PART 3: DAILY ROUTINES (Present Simple) #

🎯 Goal: Talk about daily activities Step 1: Choose 3 verbs:

  • wake up
  • cook
  • work
  • clean
  • watch TV

Write 3 sentences about yourself.

Step 2: Write 3 sentences about your dad (Don’t forget to use the 3rd person rule).

  • He __________ in the morning.
  • He __________ in the afternoon.
  • He __________ at night.

Step 3: Add time expressions

  • every day
  • in the morning
  • at night

Example: She cooks every day.

Step 4: Speak Read your sentences 3 times. Use Natural Reader if you have any doubts about the pronunciation. https://www.naturalreaders.com/online/

PART 4: PAST EVENTS (Simple Past) #

🎯 Goal: Talk about your weekend Step 1: Choose 3 actions

  • watched TV
  • studied
  • worked
  • visited family
  • played

Step 2: Complete:

  • Last __________, I __________.
  • Then, I __________.
  • After that, I __________.
  • Finally, _________.

Step 3: Speak slowly

Focus on -ed pronunciation. Use Natural Reader if you have any doubts about the pronunciation. https://www.naturalreaders.com/online/

Repeat 3 times.

PART 5: FREE TIME ACTIVITIES #

🎯 Goal: Talk about hobbies Step 1: Choose 3 activities that you like (You can choose from the list below or find your own verbs):

  • watch TV
  • listen to music
  • read
  • play sports
  • go out

Step 2: Write:

  • In my free time, I like __________.
  • I also love __________.
  • Sometimes I enjoy __________.

Step 3: Speak naturally

Try to connect ideas:

  • 👉 “In my free time, I like to watch TV and listen to music.”
  • 👉 “Sometimes, I enjoy playing sports and reading a book.”

PART 6: SPELLING PRACTICE #

🎯 Goal: Spell words clearly (letter by letter) Step 1: Practice letters

Say the alphabet once.

Step 2: Break words into letters

Example format: A-C-K-N-O-W-L-E-D-G-E

Step 3: Practice with the words below, use the “voice to text” option in your phone to dictate the spelling of each word (3 times each)

  • Beautifully
  • Administration
  • Frightened
  • Apologize
  • Experiment
  • Chemistry
  • Kindheartedly
  • Responsibility
  • Vegetable
  • Sympathetically

👉 Tip: Go slow and clear, not fast. If your phone recognizes the letters and writes the word correctly, you can confirm you did it right. You can increase your pace as long as you still spell correctly.

PART 7: NUMBERS & DATES #

🎯 Goal: Read numbers correctly Step 1: Practice numbers

10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 Focus on:

  • 13 vs 30
  • 14 vs 40

Step 2: Practice reading

  • 11 - eleven
  • 25 - twenty-five
  • 70 - seventy

👉 Use the “voice to text” option in your phone to dictate the numbers. If your phone recognizes the numbers and writes them correctly, you can confirm you did it right.

Step 3: Dates structure

👉 Month + day + year

Example:

02/10/2000 - February tenth, two thousand

Step 4: Practice 3 times each

  • 06/01/1937
  • 12/10/2024
  • 09/11/2001
  • 02/17/1996

PART 8: READING SENTENCES #

🎯 Goal: Improve pronunciation, intonation & clarity Step 1: Read the sentences below, making pauses ( / ). Example: There are / many students / in my class.

  • There is one woman in the office.
  • There are many people in my street.
  • There are two children at home.
  • There is a refrigerator in the kitchen.

Step 2: Focus on:

  • plural -s (women, children)
  • clear pronunciation

Step 3: Read slowly, then naturally (Normal speed)

PART 9: ADDRESS READING #

🎯 Goal: Read full words (no abbreviations)

  • Step 1: Practice full forms
  • St – Street
  • Rd – Road
  • Ave – Avenue
  • NY – New York
  • SD – South Dakota

Step 2: Practice structure

House number + Street name + City + State + Zip code

Step 3: Read clearly

👉 Example pattern: 32 Heather Ct. Greenwood, SC 29646 (Thirty two Heather Court. Greenwood, South Carolina two nine six four six)

  • 7227 Church St. Rockledge, FL 32955
  • 18 Garfield Ave. Mays Landing, NJ 08330
  • 725 Lilac Ln. Roselle, IL 60172
  • 58 Fairview Rd. Wausau, WI 54401
  • 8389 Briarwood Dr. Grayslake, PA 60030

PART 10: FINAL PRACTICE (VERY IMPORTANT) #

🎯 Combine everything

  • Introduce yourself
  • Describe a place or a person
  • Talk about your daily routine and a family member’s routine
  • Talk about your Christmas last year
  • Talk about your hobbies

👉 Speak for 1–2 minutes total

⭐ SIMPLE RULES FOR SUCCESS

  • Speak slowly and clearly
  • Use short sentences
  • Don’t stop — continue speaking
  • Practice out loud, not silently
