- ✅ Master **Simple Present** with _"to be"_ (am/is/are) and other verbs for descriptions, habits, and routines — in affirmative, negative, and question forms.

## 📖 Introduction

Today you will describe people and things using **adjectives** and the verb **"to be"** correctly. You will talk about **daily habits and truths** with other verbs. Finally, you will show ownership using **possessives** for real-life conversations like work or family talk.

---

## 📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary

| Term                             | Definition                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Simple Present (Verb To Be)**  | _am / is / are_ for descriptions, professions, age, nationalities → _"I am tall."_                                        |
| **Simple Present (Other Verbs)** | Base verb for habits, routines, or truths → _"She walks."_                                                                |
| **Verb To Be + Adjectives**      | Links subject to qualities after _am/is/are_ or before nouns → _"He is tall"_ / _"tall man"_                              |
| **Possessive Adjectives**        | _my, your, his, her, its, our, their_ — show belonging before nouns                                                       |
| **Auxiliary Verbs**              | **Do** = I, we, they, you / **Does** = she, it, he (for other verbs) — no auxiliary needed for _"be"_ negatives/questions |

## 🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation

### 🔵 1. Simple Present — Verb To Be

| Form              | Structure                           | Example             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **Affirmative**   | Subject + _to be_ + Adjective       | _I am intelligent._ |
| **Negative**      | Subject + _to be_ + not + Adjective | _She is not happy._ |
| **Interrogative** | _To be_ + Subject + Complement      | _Are they hungry?_  |

**Contractions you can use:**

| Full form    | Contraction                  |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- |
| I am not     | **I'm not**                  |
| She is not   | **She's not** / **isn't**    |
| They are not | **They're not** / **aren't** |

**Short Answers:**

| Question           | Yes                | No                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | -------------------- |
| _Are they hungry?_ | Yes, they **are**. | No, they **aren't**. |
| _Is she tired?_    | Yes, she **is**.   | No, she **isn't**.   |

---

### 🟢 2. Simple Present — Other Verbs

| Form              | Structure                            | Example                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| **Affirmative**   | Subject + base verb + complement     | _I eat breakfast in the morning._       |
| **Negative**      | Subject + do/does + not + complement | _I don't watch TV in the afternoon._    |
| **Interrogative** | Do/Does + subject + complement       | _Do you have crayons in your backpack?_ |

**When to use Do / Does:**

| Auxiliary | Subjects         |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| **Do**    | I, you, we, they |
| **Does**  | He, she, it      |

> 💡 _Remember: with_ **does\***, the main verb keeps its base form — no "s" added.*
> ✅ *She **does** walk.* / ❌ ~~*She does walks.\*~~

---

### 🟡 3. Verb To Be + Adjectives

Adjectives can go in **2 positions**:

| Position       | Structure   | Example              |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------------------- |
| **Position 1** | After "be"  | _I am **happy**._    |
| **Position 2** | Before noun | _a **happy** person_ |

> ⚠️ **Important rules:**
>
> - Adjectives **never** use plural form → ❌ ~~_bigs apples_~~ ✅ _big apples_
> - Adjectives always describe nouns

---

### 🟠 4. Possessive Adjectives

Match the possessive adjective to the owner:

| Pronoun | Possessive | Example      |
| ------- | ---------- | ------------ |
| I       | **my**     | _my car_     |
| You     | **your**   | _your bag_   |
| He      | **his**    | _his book_   |
| She     | **her**    | _her house_  |
| It      | **its**    | _its color_  |
| We      | **our**    | _our school_ |
| They    | **their**  | _their cars_ |

> ⚠️ **Rules to remember:**
>
> - Always use possessive adjectives **before a noun** → ✅ _"my car"_
> - ❌ ~~_"mine car"_~~ / ❌ ~~_"my is"_~~
> - They **do not change** for the noun's number or gender

## 💡 Examples

### Verb To Be

> _"I am a teacher."_
> _"She is not tired."_
> _"Are they happy? — Yes, they are."_

### Other Verbs

> _"I play soccer."_
> _"They don't work late."_
> _"Do you live here? — Yes, I do."_

### Be + Adjectives

> _"My cat is cute."_ / _"I have a cute cat."_
> _"She is smart."_ / _"She is a smart student."_

### Possessives

> _"This is my book."_
> _"Her house is big."_
> _"Their cars are new."_

### Combined

> _"I am your student."_
> _"My teacher is nice."_
> _"She teaches every day."_

## 📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember

> **Before you go, make sure you know:**

1. **Verb To Be** — Use _am / is / are_ for descriptions. Adjectives go **after** the verb _to be_ or **before** nouns.
2. **Other Verbs** — Use base form for I/you/they/we. Use _do/does_ for negatives and questions.
3. **Possessives** — _my / your / his / her / its / our / their_ always go **before** a noun.
4. **Daily practice** — _"My family is happy. We eat breakfast together."_
