## 🎯 Learning Objectives

> **At the end of the class, you will be able to:**

- ✅ Form **Simple Present 3rd person singular** sentences and apply spelling rules correctly (+s / +es / +ies).
- ✅ Use **subject, object, and reflexive pronouns** correctly.
- ✅ Use **frequency adverbs** (always, sometimes, rarely…) and **daily routine vocabulary** to describe habits and answer *"How often...?"* questions fluently.

---

## 📖 Introduction

Today you will learn the **3rd person Simple Present** rules with pronouns and nouns for talking about other people's actions and routines — *"She always wakes herself up"* — for work schedules, family habits, or daily life conversations. Finally, you will use **indefinite and definite frequency adverbs** to say how often things happen.

---

## 📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **3rd Person Singular (pronouns)** | He / She / It → verbs add **-s** *(walks)*, **-es** *(goes)*, **-ies** *(cries)* |
| **3rd Person Singular (nouns)** | My family, His mom, The doctor, Her sister → *(plays, does, tries)* |
| **Subject Pronouns** | Do the action: *I, you, he, she, it, we, they* |
| **Object Pronouns** | Receive the action: *me, you, him, her, it, us, them* |
| **Reflexive Pronouns** | Refer back to the subject: *myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves* |
| **Frequency Adverbs** | Always (100%) / Usually (90%) / Often (80%) / Sometimes (50%) / Rarely–Hardly ever (10%) / Never (0%) |
| **Definite Frequency** | Every day / daily, every month / monthly, twice a week |
| **Every day vs. Everyday** | *Every day* = each day (adverb) / *Everyday* = common, ordinary (adjective: *everyday clothes*) |
| **Daily Routine** | Wake up → brush teeth → eat breakfast → go to work / school |

## 🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation

### 🔵 1. Simple Present — 3rd Person Singular

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | He/She/It + verb **-s/-es/-ies** | *She **walks** to work.* |
| **Negative** | He/She/It + **does not** + base verb | *She **doesn't walk** to work.* |
| **Interrogative** | **Does** + subject + base verb? | *Does she **walk** to work?* |

**Spelling rules for 3rd person singular:**

| Rule | When to use | Examples |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **+ s** | Most verbs | *walk → walk**s*** / *eat → eat**s*** |
| **+ es** | Verbs ending in **o, ch, sh, s, th, z** | *go → go**es*** / *watch → watch**es*** |
| **y → ies** | Verbs ending in **consonant + y** | *cry → cr**ies*** / *fly → fl**ies*** |

> 💡 *If the verb ends in **vowel + y**, just add -s:* play → play**s**


---

### 🟡 2. Frequency Adverbs

#### Indefinite Frequency

| Adverb | Frequency | Position |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Always** | 100% | Before main verb / After "be" |
| **Usually** | 90% | Before main verb / After "be" |
| **Often** | 80% | Before main verb / After "be" |
| **Sometimes** | 50% | Before main verb / After "be" |
| **Rarely / Hardly ever** | 10% | Before main verb / After "be" |
| **Never** | 0% | Before main verb / After "be" |

> ✅ *She **always eats** breakfast.* (before main verb)
> ✅ *He **is never** late.* (after "be")

#### Definite Frequency — at the **end** of the sentence

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| Every day / daily | *She exercises **every day**.* |
| Once a week | *He goes to the gym **once a week**.* |
| Twice a month | *We meet **twice a month**.* |
| Every Monday | *She calls her mom **every Monday**.* |

**Asking and answering "How often...?"**

> *"How often does she exercise?"*
> → *"She exercises **twice a week**."*

---

### 🟠 3. Daily Routine

The natural sequence of a daily routine:

| Step | Verb | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | **Wake up** | *She wakes up at 6:00 a.m.* |
| 2 | **Get up** | *He gets up at 6:15.* |
| 3 | **Take a shower** | *She takes a shower.* |
| 4 | **Have / Eat breakfast** | *He eats breakfast.* ❌ ~~*takes breakfast*~~ |
| 5 | **Go to work / school** | *She goes to work at 8:00.* |
| 6 | **Have / Eat dinner** | *He has dinner at 7:00 p.m.* |
| 7 | **Go to bed** | *She goes to bed at 10:00.* |

> ⚠️ For meals always use **have** or **eat** — never *"take breakfast"*.

## 💡 Examples

### 3rd Person Singular
> *"She **works** late."* (+s)
> *"It **goes** fast."* (+es)
> *"He **cries**."* (y → ies)
> *"Does she work? — No, she doesn't."*

### Pronouns
> **Subject:** *"**She** eats lunch at noon."*
> **Object:** *"I like **her**."*
> **Reflexive:** *"She dresses **herself**."*

### Routine + Frequency
> *"He **always** wakes up at 6."*
> *"She brushes **her** teeth every morning."*
> *"We **never** skip breakfast."*

### Combined
> *"My sister (**she**) always wakes **herself** up (reflexive). I see **her** (object) every day."*

## 📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember

> **Before you go, make sure you know:**

1. **3rd Person** — Add *-s / -es / -ies* in affirmative; use *does / doesn't + base verb* for negatives and questions.
2. **Pronouns** — **Subject** *(he runs)* does the action / **Object** *(see him)* receives the action / **Reflexive** *(hurt himself)* refers back to the subject.
3. **Frequency Adverbs** — *Always / usually / often / sometimes / never* go **before** the main verb or **after** "be".
4. **Daily Routine** — Wake up → breakfast *(eat/have)* → work. Answer *"How often does she...?"* with definite expressions like *"every day"* or *"twice a week"*.
