## 📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary

### 🔵 1. Prepositions of Time

We have **3 different prepositions of time**: **at**, **in**, and **on**.

---

#### ⏰ AT — Specific time or holiday period

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Specific time | *I get up **at** 7 o'clock.* |
| Specific time | *The movie starts **at** 8:30.* |
| Holiday period | *They sing carols **at** Christmas.* |
| Holiday period | *Come and visit us **at** Thanksgiving.* |

---

#### 📅 ON — Days of the week and specific dates

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Day of the week | *I will visit you **on** Wednesday.* |
| Day of the week | *Where were you **on** Friday?* |
| Specific date | *His birthday is **on** March 27th.* |
| Specific date | *The exam is **on** the 16th.* |

---

#### 📆 IN — Months, years, decades, centuries, seasons & time periods

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Month | *My birthday is **in** November.* |
| Year | *The Titanic sank **in** 1912.* |
| Decade | *There were many hippies **in** the '60s.* |
| Century | *We are living **in** the 21st century.* |
| Season | *We go to the beach **in** summer.* |
| Time period | *The meeting starts **in** 10 minutes.* |

---

> 💡 **Quick reference:**
>
> | Preposition | Use for |
> |-------------|---------|
> | **AT** | Specific times & holidays |
> | **ON** | Days & specific dates |
> | **IN** | Months, years, seasons, centuries |

### 🟢 2. Prepositions of Place

We use prepositions of place to indicate the **location or position** of an object, person, or place.

They normally follow **2 sentence patterns**:

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Pattern 1** | The + noun + verb + preposition + the + noun | *The cat is **on** the sofa.* |
| **Pattern 2** | There + verb + a/an + noun + preposition + the + noun | *There is a cat **on** the sofa.* / *There are two cats **on** the sofa.* |

**Most common prepositions of place:**

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **in** | inside | *The book is **in** the bag.* |
| **on** | on top of / on a surface | *The cup is **on** the table.* |
| **under** | below | *The cat is **under** the chair.* |
| **behind** | at the back | *The ball is **behind** the door.* |
| **in front of** | facing | *She is **in front of** the class.* |
| **next to / beside** | at the side | *The lamp is **next to** the bed.* |
| **between** | in the middle of two things | *The bank is **between** the school and the park.* |
| **above** | higher than | *The clock is **above** the TV.* |
| **below** | lower than | *The shoes are **below** the shelf.* |

### 🟡 3. Demonstrative Adjectives

Demonstrative adjectives are used to **identify nouns** based on their **distance** from the speaker. There are 4: **this, these, that, those**.

| | **Near** (can see, touch & point) | **Far** (can see & point, but NOT touch) |
|-|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Singular** | **THIS** | **THAT** |
| **Plural** | **THESE** | **THOSE** |

---

#### 👆 THIS — Singular, near

> *"**This** is my cat."* *(I can see it, touch it, and point to it.)*
> *"**This** dog is big."*

#### 👆👆 THESE — Plural, near

> *"**These** apples are red."* *(I can see them, touch them, and point to them.)*
> *"**These** are my brothers."*

#### 👉 THAT — Singular, far

> *"**That** car over there is mine."* *(I can see it and point to it, but I can't touch it.)*
> *"**That** is my mom."*

#### 👉👉 THOSE — Plural, far

> *"**Those** notebooks on the table are ours."* *(I can see them and point to them, but I can't touch them.)*
> *"**Those** are big trees."*

> 💡 *In English, demonstrative adjectives can refer to **people** too — it is not considered impolite.*

### 🔴 5. Adverbs of Manner

We use adverbs of manner to describe a **verb (an action)** — how somebody does something.

> *"He drives **carefully**."*
> *"My mother speaks **slowly**."*
> *"The nurses left the room **quietly**."*

---

#### 5.1 How to form an adverb of manner

Normally, add **-ly** to an adjective:

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| happy | **happily** |
| careful | **carefully** |
| quick | **quickly** |
| slow | **slowly** |
| beautiful | **beautifully** |
| sad | **sadly** |

---

---

#### 5.2 Irregular Adverbs — must be memorized!

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| good | **well** |
| hard | **hard** |
| fast | **fast** |
| late | **late** |
| straight | **straight** |
| high | **high** |

> ⚠️ *These adverbs do **not** add -ly — they keep the same form or change completely.*

---

#### 5.3 Adjectives vs. Adverbs

| Type | Describes | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Adjective** | A **noun** (usually with verb "to be") | *She is **beautiful**.* |
| **Adverb** | An **action / verb** | *She sings **beautifully**.* |

> 💡 *Adjectives describe **what** something is. Adverbs describe **how** something is done.*
