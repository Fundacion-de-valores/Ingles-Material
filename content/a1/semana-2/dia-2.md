## 🎯 Learning Objectives

> **At the end of the class, you will be able to:**

- ✅ Use **Simple Past with "to be"** (was/were) in affirmative, negative, and question forms.
- ✅ Use **Simple Past with other verbs** (regular & irregular) for completed actions — affirmative, negative, interrogative, and short answers.
- ✅ Focus on **pronunciation** and use past tense for **storytelling** and past events.

## 📖 Introduction

Today you will use the **Simple Past tense** to talk about finished actions — *"I went yesterday."* As A1 students, use it for recapping weekend plans or sharing life experiences beyond present routines.

## 📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary

### 🔵 Simple Past — Verb "To Be" (Was / Were)

| Pronoun | Past form |
|---------|-----------|
| I / He / She / It | **was** |
| You / We / They | **were** |

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | Subject + *was/were* + complement | *I was at home.* |
| **Negative** | Subject + *was/were* + **not** + complement | *She was not happy.* |
| **Yes/No Question** | *Was/Were* + subject + complement + ? | *Were you at the party?* |
| **Open-ended Question** | Wh-word + *was/were* + subject + complement + ? | *Where were you last night?* |

**Short Answers:**

| Question | Yes | No |
|----------|-----|----|
| *Were you at the party?* | Yes, I **was**. | No, I **was not** / **wasn't**. |
| *Was it great?* | Yes, it **was**. | No, it **wasn't**. |
| *Were they there?* | Yes, they **were**. | No, they **weren't**. |

---

### 🟢 Simple Past — Other Verbs

| Type | How to form | Examples |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Regular** | Base verb + **-ed** | *walk → walk**ed*** / *watch → watch**ed*** |
| **Irregular** | Change form — memorize | *go → **went*** / *eat → **ate*** / *see → **saw*** |

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | Subject + past verb + complement | *She watched a movie.* |
| **Negative** | Subject + **did not** + base verb + complement | *He didn't sleep well.* |
| **Yes/No Question** | **Did** + subject + base verb + complement + ? | *Did you do your homework?* |
| **Open-ended Question** | Wh-word + **did** + subject + base verb + ? | *Where did you go?* |

**Short Answers:**

| Question | Yes | No |
|----------|-----|----|
| *Did you do your homework?* | Yes, I **did**. | No, I **didn't**. |
| *Did she help you yesterday?* | Yes, she **did**. | No, she **didn't**. |

---

### 🟡 Past Tense Pronunciation

| Element | Pronunciation tip |
|---------|------------------|
| **Was** | Often reduced → /wəz/ |
| **Were** | Often reduced → /wər/ |
| **Regular verbs -ed** | /t/ / /d/ / /ɪd/ *(see Day 1 Week 2)* |
| **Irregular verbs** | Memorize each pronunciation individually |

---

### 🟠 Did + Base Verb

| Use | Structure | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| **Negative** | Subject + **didn't** + base verb | *My sister **didn't go** to the party.* |
| **Question** | **Did** + subject + base verb + ? | ***Did** you see that?* |

> ⚠️ After **did / didn't** always use the **base form** of the verb — never the past form.
> ✅ *Did she **go**?* / ❌ ~~*Did she **went**?*~~

---

### 🔴 Subject Questions

When asking **about the subject** (who did the action), use the **past verb directly** — no *did* needed:

| Type | Question | Answer |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Subject question** | *Who **bought** this?* | *John **bought** it.* |
| **Object question** | *Who did you **see**?* | *I **saw** my sister.* |

> 💡 *If "who/what" is the subject → use past verb directly.*
> *If "who/what" is the object → use did + base verb.*

## 🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation

### 🔵 1. Simple Past — Was / Were (Full Summary)

| | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|-|-------------|----------|---------|
| **I** | I **was** tired. | I **wasn't** ready. | **Was** I late? |
| **He/She/It** | She **was** home. | He **wasn't** there. | **Was** it good? |
| **You/We/They** | They **were** happy. | We **weren't** late. | **Were** you there? |

**Natural speech reductions:**
> *"He was mad"* → pronounced **/hɪwəz/**
> *"Where was he?"* → pronounced **/wɛrwəzhɛ/**

---

### 🟢 2. Simple Past — Other Verbs (Full Summary)

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | Subject + past form | *She **walked** to the park.* / *We **went** home.* |
| **Negative** | Subject + **didn't** + base verb | *She **didn't walk** to the park.* |
| **Yes/No Question** | **Did** + subject + base verb + ? | ***Did** you **drink** soda?* |
| **Short answer** | Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**. | |
| **Open-ended Question** | Wh-word + **did** + subject + base verb | *Where **did** you **go**?* |
| **Subject Question** | Who/What + past verb | *Who **went** to the party?* |

---

## 💡 Examples

### Was / Were
> *"I **was** late."*
> *"They **weren't** home."*
> *"**Were** you there? — Yes, we **were**."*

### Other Verbs
> *"She **watched** a movie."* (regular)
> *"We **ate** dinner."* (irregular)
> *"He **didn't sleep** well."*
> *"**Did** they play? — Yes, they **did**."*

### Subject vs. Object Questions
> **Object:** *"Who did you **see**? — I **saw** John."*
> **Subject:** *"Who **came**? — John **came**."*

### Combined
> *"Yesterday, I **was** tired. I **didn't eat** lunch. What **did** you do? — I **went** home."*

## ✏️ Practical Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Was or Were?
Fill in the blank with *was* or *were*:

> *"I ___ at school yesterday."* → **was**
> *"They ___ not at the party."* → **were**
> *"___ she tired after work?"* → **Was**
> *"We ___ happy to see you."* → **were**

---

### Exercise 2 — Affirmative → Negative & Question
Transform these sentences:

| Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| *She went to the store.* | *She didn't ___ to the store.* | *___ she go to the store?* |
| *They ate pizza.* | *They didn't ___ pizza.* | *___ they eat pizza?* |
| *He was late.* | *He ___ late.* | *___ he late?* |

> *(Answers: go / Did — eat / Did — wasn't / Was)*

---

### Exercise 3 — Subject vs. Object Questions
Choose the correct question:

> *You saw Maria yesterday.*
> a) *Who saw you?* b) *Who did you see?* → **b) Who did you see?**

> *Maria called you.*
> a) *Who called you?* b) *Who did call you?* → **a) Who called you?**

---

### Exercise 4 — Storytelling Activity
Tell a short story about **yesterday** using at least:
- **3 sentences** with *was/were*
- **3 sentences** with other past verbs (regular + irregular)
- **1 question** with *did*

> 🎙️ *Example: "Yesterday I was very tired. I didn't go to the gym. I ate dinner at home and watched TV. Were you busy too? What did you do?"*

> **Record yourself** and share with the class!

## 📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember

> **Before you go, make sure you know:**

1. **Was / Were:**
   - *I / He / She / It* → **was** / **wasn't**
   - *You / We / They* → **were** / **weren't**

2. **Other Verbs — Affirmative:** Use the **past form** → *walked / went / ate*

3. **Other Verbs — Negative:** **Didn't** + **base form** of the verb → *She didn't go*

4. **Other Verbs — Questions:**
   - Yes/No → **Did** + subject + base verb → *Did you go?*
   - Open-ended → **Wh-word** + **did** + subject + base verb → *Where did you go?*
