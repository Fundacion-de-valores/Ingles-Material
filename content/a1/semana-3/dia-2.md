## 🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation

### 🔵 1. Present Continuous — Actions Happening Now

Used for actions happening **at the moment of speaking**.

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | Subject + **am/is/are** + verb **-ing** | *I **am studying** now.* |
| **Negative** | Subject + **am/is/are not** + verb **-ing** | *She **is not working** right now.* |
| **Interrogative** | **Am/Is/Are** + subject + verb **-ing**? | ***Are** they **eating**?* |

> 💡 Signal words for "now": *now, right now, at the moment, currently, look! / listen!*

---

### 🟢 2. Present Continuous — Future Arrangements

Used for **fixed plans** in the near future — usually with a specific time or person.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | *I **am meeting** my boss tomorrow.* |
| **Negative** | *She **is not traveling** tonight.* |
| **Interrogative** | ***Are** you **working** this weekend?* |

> 💡 The plan was **already arranged** before the moment of speaking — it is not a spontaneous decision.
> Compare: *"I **will help** you."* (decision NOW) vs. *"I **am meeting** her tomorrow."* (arranged BEFORE)

---

### 🟡 3. Forming -ing Verbs

| Rule | When to use | Examples |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **+ ing** | Most verbs | *work → work**ing*** / *eat → eat**ing*** |
| **Drop -e + ing** | Verbs ending in **-e** | *make → mak**ing*** / *have → hav**ing*** |
| **Double consonant + ing** | Short verbs ending in consonant-vowel-consonant | *run → runn**ing*** / *sit → sitt**ing*** |

> ⚠️ Never add -ing to a verb without **am / is / are**: ❌ *I studying* → ✅ *I **am** studying*

---

### 🟠 4. Time Expressions

| When | Expressions |
|------|------------|
| **Now** | *now, right now, at the moment, currently* |
| **Future** | *tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, on Monday* |

## 💡 Examples

### Present Continuous — Now
> *"I **am studying** right now."*
> *"She **is working** at the moment."*
> *"They **are eating** lunch now."*

### Present Continuous — Future Arrangements
> *"I **am meeting** my boss tomorrow."*
> *"She **is traveling** next week."*
> *"We **are having** a meeting tonight."*

### Time Expressions
> *"I **am working** now."*
> *"They **are studying** tonight."*
> *"He **is calling** you tomorrow."*

### -ing Verb Forms
> *"She **is making** dinner."* (make → making)
> *"He **is running** in the park."* (run → running)
> *"We **are studying** English."* (study → studying)

## 📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember

> **Before you go, make sure you know:**

1. **Present Continuous (Now)** — *am/is/are + verb(-ing)* for actions happening now. (*I am working now.*)
2. **Present Continuous (Future)** — Used for planned arrangements with a specific time. (*I am meeting her tomorrow.*)
3. **Time Expressions** — Now → *now, right now, at the moment*; Future → *tonight, tomorrow, next week*.
4. **-ing Form** — Add *-ing* (work → working), drop *-e* (make → making), double consonant (run → running).

> ⚠️ **Common mistakes to avoid:**
> ❌ *I am study now.* → ✅ *I am **studying** now.*
> ❌ *I meeting my friend tomorrow.* → ✅ *I **am meeting** my friend tomorrow.*
