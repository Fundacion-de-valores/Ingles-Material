## 📚 Important Terms — Daily Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Singular Noun** | One item (*book, apple, child*) |
| **Plural Noun** | More than one (*books, apples, children*) |
| **Count Nouns** | Nouns you can count — have singular and plural forms (*apple / apples, car / cars*) |
| **Non-Count Nouns** | Nouns you cannot count individually — no plural form (*water, money, information*) |
| **Quantifiers** | Words that express quantity (*some, any, much, many, a lot of, a few, a little*) |
| **Containers / Expressions** | Used with non-count nouns (*a bottle of water, a cup of coffee, a piece of information*) |

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts — Detailed Explanation

### 🔵 1. Singular and Plural Nouns

| Type | Rule | Examples |
|------|------|---------|
| **Regular plurals** | Add **-s** | *book → book**s*** / *car → car**s*** |
| **-es plurals** | Verbs ending in **-ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o** | *watch → watch**es*** / *potato → potato**es*** |
| **-y → -ies** | Consonant + **y** | *city → cit**ies*** / *baby → bab**ies*** |
| **Irregular plurals** | Must be memorized | *child → **children*** / *man → **men*** / *person → **people*** |

---

### 🟢 2. Count vs. Non-Count Nouns

| | Count Nouns | Non-Count Nouns |
|-|-------------|----------------|
| **Definition** | Can be counted individually | Cannot be counted individually |
| **Singular form** | ✅ *an apple / a car* | ❌ ~~a water~~ |
| **Plural form** | ✅ *apples / cars* | ❌ ~~waters / moneys~~ |
| **Examples** | apple, book, job, student, idea | water, money, information, rice, music |

> ⚠️ **Common non-count nouns to remember:** *water, coffee, tea, rice, bread, money, time, information, advice, news, work, music*

---

### 🟡 3. Using "a / an" and "some"

| Article / Word | Use | Example |
|---------------|-----|---------|
| **a / an** | Singular count nouns | *a book / an apple* |
| **some** | Plural count nouns | *some apples* |
| **some** | Non-count nouns | *some water* |

---

### 🟠 4. Quantifiers

| Quantifier | Use with | Example |
|------------|----------|---------|
| **Many** | Count nouns | *There are **many** students.* |
| **A few** | Count nouns | *I have **a few** friends.* |
| **Much** | Non-count nouns | *There is **much** work to do.* |
| **A little** | Non-count nouns | *I have **a little** money left.* |
| **A lot of** | Both count & non-count | *I have **a lot of** books. / **a lot of** time.* |
| **Some** | Both (affirmative) | *I need **some** apples. / **some** water.* |
| **Any** | Both (negative / questions) | *I don't have **any** apples. / Do you have **any** water?* |

---

### 🔴 5. Containers / Expressions of Quantity

Used to measure non-count nouns:

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **a bottle of** | *a bottle of water* |
| **a cup of** | *a cup of coffee* |
| **a piece of** | *a piece of information / a piece of bread* |
| **a bowl of** | *a bowl of rice* |
| **a glass of** | *a glass of juice* |

---

## 💡 Examples

### Singular and Plural Nouns
> *"I have one **book**."*
> *"I have three **books**."*
> *"She has two **children**."*

### Count vs. Non-Count Nouns
> *"I have **an apple**."* (count — singular)
> *"I have **some apples**."* (count — plural)
> *"I have **some water**."* (non-count)

### Quantifiers
> *"There are **many** students in the class."*
> *"I have **a few** friends."*
> *"There is **a little** money left."*
> *"We have **a lot of** work today."*

### Containers / Expressions
> *"I drink **a bottle of water** every day."*
> *"She eats **a bowl of rice**."*
> *"He needs **a piece of information**."*

## 📝 Summary — Key Points to Remember

> **Before you go, make sure you know:**

1. **Singular & Plural** — Add *-s / -es* for most plurals; some are irregular (*child → children, man → men*).
2. **Count Nouns** — Can be counted, have singular & plural (*apple/apples, car/cars*).
3. **Non-Count Nouns** — Cannot be counted, no plural (*water, money, information*).
4. **Quantifiers** — Count → *many, a few*; Non-count → *much, a little*; Both → *some, a lot of, any*.
5. **Containers** — Use expressions to measure non-count nouns: *a bottle of water, a cup of coffee, a piece of information*.

> ⚠️ **Common mistakes to avoid:**
> ❌ *informations, moneys* → ✅ *information, money*
> ❌ *much apples* → ✅ *many apples*
> ❌ *a little books* → ✅ *a few books*
