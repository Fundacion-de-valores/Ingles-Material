## TOPIC 1 - MILITARY (PHONETIC) ALPHABET

_The Military Alphabet, also known as the NATO Phonetic Alphabet, is an international spelling system used to communicate letters clearly over the phone or radio._
_Each letter has a unique word to avoid confusion._
**Example:**
- *A = Alpha* 
- *B = Bravo*
- *C = Charlie* 
- *D = Delta*

_It is commonly used in Customer Service, Aviation, Emergency Services, and the Military._

## TOPIC 3 - SIMPLE PRESENT

### The Simple Present is used to talk about:

- *Daily routine*
- *Habits*
- *Facts*
- *General truths*
- *Scheduled events*

**Structure**

**Affirmative**
- *Subject + Verb + Complement*
- *Example:*
- *I work from home.*
- *She works in Customer Service.*
---

**Negative**
- *Subject + do/does + not + Verb*
- *Example:*
- *I do not work weekends.*
- *He doesn't answer billing questions.*
---

**Question**
- *Do/Does + Subject + Verb?*
- *Example:*
- *Do you work on Saturdays?*
- *Does she speak English?*
---
