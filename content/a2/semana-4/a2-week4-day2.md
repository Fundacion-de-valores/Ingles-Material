## 🎯Theme of the Day
**Talking About Experiences and Hypothetical Situations**
### Learning Objectives
_Students will:_
- *Identify Present Perfect questions.*
- *Recognize Second Conditional questions.*
- *Express experiences naturally.*
- *Explain ideas with more detail.*
- *Improve fluency while answering unexpected questions.*

## 📖 Introduction
Today you will learn how to answer questions about your life experiences and imaginary situations. These questions require more than remembering grammar rules. You will practice organizing your ideas and explaining your answers clearly.

## 🔑 Key Concepts
### How to recognize Present Perfect
_Look for:_
- *Have you ever...*
- *Have you...*
- *Has he...*
- *already*
- *yet*
- *since*
- *for*

## Example:
_Have you ever traveled outside your country?_

### How to recognize Second Conditional
_Look for:_
- *If...*
- *would*
- *could*
- *should*
- *might*

## Example:
_What would you do if you had one million dollars?_

## Talking about Experiences
_Useful expressions_
- *I have never...*
- *I have already...*
- *I have just...*
- *I have always wanted to...*
- *One time I...*

## Expressing Opinions
- *I think...*
- *In my opinion...*
- *I believe...*
- *Personally...*
- *For me…*

## Making Answers Longer
- *Answer*
- *Reason*
- *Example*
- *Opinion*
- *Instead of:*
- *"I would travel."*

**Say:**

_"I would travel to Japan because I love learning about different cultures and I have always wanted to visit that country."_
