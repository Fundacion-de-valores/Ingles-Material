## 🎯 Theme of the Day
### Certification Readiness
### Learning Objectives
### Students will:
1. *Demonstrate confidence while answering different types of questions.*
2. *Recognize the grammar required in each question.*
3. *Improve reading comprehension strategies.*
4. *Review the most common grammar mistakes.*
5. *Communicate ideas naturally and confidently.*

## 📖 Introduction
Today you will combine everything you have practiced throughout the week. The goal is to answer questions confidently, recognize grammar patterns quickly, and communicate your ideas naturally throughout the certification.
### 📚 Important Vocabulary
- *Daily life*
- *Work*
- *Travel*
- *Education*
- *Family*
- *Health*
- *Technology*
- *Environment*
- *Personal experiences*
- *Future plans*

## 🔑 Key Concepts
### Before Answering
- *Read or listen carefully.*
- *Identify the tense.*
- *Identify the question word.*
- *Think about the subject.*
- *Answer using the same tense.*
- *Add supporting information.*

## Reading Strategies
- *Read the questions first.*
- *Identify key words.*
- *Look for synonyms.*
- *Do not translate every word.*
- *Focus on the main idea.*

## Grammar Strategies
- *Identify the subject.*
- *Identify the auxiliary verb.*
- *Recognize time expressions.*
- *Pay attention to irregular verbs.*
- *Check word order in questions.*

## Speaking Tips
- *Speak confidently.*
- *Do not panic if you make a mistake.*
- *Correct yourself naturally.*
- *Avoid memorized answers.*
- *Keep eye contact if the interview is online.*
- *Always answer with complete sentences.*

## ✏️ To practice:

### Wednesday sample questions
**Personal Information**

- *Tell me about yourself.*
- *Describe your family.*
- *What do you do in your free time?*
- *Tell me about your hometown.*
- *What are your strengths?*

**Mixed Tenses**

- *What do you usually do after work?*
- *What did you do last weekend?*
- *What are you going to do next weekend?*
- *Have you ever traveled alone?*
- *What would you do if you had a free month to travel?*

**Describing Experiences**

- *Tell me about a memorable vacation.*
- *Describe your favorite restaurant.*
- *Tell me about your best friend.*
- *Describe your daily routine.*
- *Tell me about a difficult day you had recently.*

**Giving Opinions**

- *What do you think makes a good employee?*
- *Do you prefer studying online or in person? Why?*
- *What is more important: experience or education?*
- *Should everyone learn English? Why?*
- *What advice would you give someone who is learning English?*

**Follow-up Questions**
- *Why?*
- *Can you explain that?*
- *Can you give me an example?*
- *Tell me more.*
- *What happened next?*
- *How did you feel?*
- *What was the most difficult part?*
- *Would you do it again? Why?*
- *What did you learn from that experience?*
- *Is there anything else you would like to add?*

**Bonus:** _"Rapid Fire" Questions_

These can be used on **any day** as warm-ups or speaking drills.
- *Chocolate or tea? Why?*
- *Cats or dogs?*
- *Summer or winter?*
- *Morning or night?*
- *Books or movies?*
- *What is your dream job?*
- *What is your favorite app?*
- *Can you cook?*
- *What is your favorite holiday?*
- *What makes you happy?*
- *Who inspires you?*
- *What is your biggest goal?*
- *What is your favorite place?*
- *What makes you laugh?*
- *What do you usually do before going to bed?*
- *What did you do this morning?*
- *What will you do after class?*
- *Have you ever learned another language?*
- *If you could live anywhere, where would you live?*
- *What would you do if you could have any superpower?*
