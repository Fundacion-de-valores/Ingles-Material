## Topics of the Day:
* Food Vocabulary
* Animals
* Clothes
* Parts of the House

## Learning Objectives:
*By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:*
1. Use basic food vocabulary to ask and answer simple questions.
2. Recognize and name common animals in visual and audio prompts.
3. Use clothing vocabulary to describe what someone is wearing in simple sentences, and to describe your own outfit as well.
4. Use basic vocabulary to describe locations within a house (e.g., kitchen, bedroom) in simple sentences.

## Introduction:
Today you will learn how to talk about everyday topics using simple English. We will practice how to communicate about things we see and use in our daily lives, like meals, animals, outfits, and places at home.
First, you will explore words related to different kinds of food and practice how to use them in basic conversations. Then, you will learn how to identify different animals through images and listening activities.
After that, you will focus on clothing and how to talk about what people are wearing, including how to describe your own outfits. Finally, we will learn how to talk about different areas inside a home and explain where things are.
By the end of the lesson, you will feel more confident understanding and using common vocabulary in simple, clear sentences.
